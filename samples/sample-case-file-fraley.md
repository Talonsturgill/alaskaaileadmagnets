## The deck (9 slides, dossier-lite)

1. COVER. "Their insurance bill fell 36 percent. The market's rose 20."
   Art direction, a dark highway plane at night, one gold telemetry line
   tracing the road, headline set huge in Fraunces. Kicker, CASE FILES.
2. THE COMPANY. A 700 truck family fleet out of Rushville Indiana, hauling
   metal, building materials, machinery across the eastern US. Deliberately
   ordinary. Art, a quiet fleet-scale grid of truck glyphs, one lit gold.
3. THE BOTTLENECK. Insurance climbing with the market, no visibility into
   driver risk, a back office buried in paper, maintenance checks slipping.
   Quote energy, the fleet could not see which of 650 drivers was about to
   cost it money. Art, four gauges, all drifting the wrong way.
4. THE BUILD. AI dash cams watching the road and the cab, telematics on
   every truck, automated coaching clips instead of ride-alongs, and an
   internal driver score they built on top. Ladder rung, a workflow with
   people in the loop, not an autonomous agent. Art, exploded anatomy of a
   cab, camera, sensor, phone, scoreboard.
5. THE RECEIPTS. Per Samsara's case study of the fleet. Insurance costs
   down 36 percent, 2020 to 2023, while the market climbed about 20.
   187,000 dollars and 6,700 hours a year back from paperwork. 75 percent
   fewer overdue maintenance checks. Total estimated savings 800,000
   dollars plus a year, the fleet's own estimate. Art, the keepable stat
   table, tabular numerals, source label ON the slide.
6. WHAT IT TOOK. Hardware on every truck, a coaching habit the safety team
   had to actually run, drivers brought along with a score they could see,
   about three years to the full insurance result. Benefits ramped, they
   did not arrive day one. Art, a timeline with the ramp drawn honestly.
7. THE CATCH. The numbers are the vendor's telling of a happy customer.
   Cameras in cabs are a trust negotiation with drivers, not a purchase.
   And none of it pays without someone owning the coaching loop, the tool
   does not manage the fleet for you. Art, the gold line interrupted where
   the human has to hold it.
8. THE TRANSLATION. Alaska fleets run the same math with harsher roads,
   longer empty legs, and winter physics. Trucking, air cargo ground ops,
   fuel delivery, school and tour fleets. The equivalent build is bought,
   not custom, a workflow rung, entry cost is per-truck hardware plus a
   monthly seat, the expensive part is the coaching habit. Where it does
   not translate, a five truck outfit's insurance line is too small to
   move 36 percent in dollars that matter. Art, the Alaska road map, gold
   route lines north.
9. CLOSE. One company. One bottleneck. One honest breakdown. Daily. The
   offer line from config. ALASKA.AI, Polaris star, alaskaaihq.com small
   in mono.
