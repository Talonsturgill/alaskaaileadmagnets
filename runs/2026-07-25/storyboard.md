## DIRECTORS ROOM VERDICT (showrunner synthesis)

Three lenses pitched. Data-journalist, field-documentarian, systems-illustrator.

**WINNER, data-journalist.** The deck's problem IS a measurement problem, and
this lens produced the sharpest and most original argument in the room, that
the vendor's number is not modest or immodest but UNMULTIPLIABLE. The blog
never states visits per week, never states methodology, and never claims
volume (c10), so the arithmetic every operator runs in their head at a vendor
meeting, twelve minutes times visits times five days, is an arithmetic the
vendor never licensed. Drawing that unlicensed multiplication and then drawing
why it stops is a slide nobody else on this feed has made. It also carries
zero WebGL risk on a ten slide deck, which is a real production argument.

**GRAFT 1, from systems-illustrator. The SPEC PLATE replaces the Source
Gutter.** The data-journalist's rotated right-margin gutter was the weakest
part of an otherwise excellent system and it flagged its own QA risk (rotated
DOM text confuses line-box collision checking). The systems-illustrator's four
row provenance plate is better content AND safer to render. Label over value,
no colons. It makes instinct i-005 structural instead of hand repeated.

MEASURED BY / INSTRUMENT / POPULATION / UNIT

**GRAFT 2, from field-documentarian. The DIE CUT PAPER CHOROPLETH is S9's
hero.** Seven boroughs punched clean through the chart paper, lamp light
falling through the holes onto the desk below. Absence drawn as absence. It is
the best single image any lens produced, it is honest, and it fits the desk
world exactly. It carries the 432px thumb read that the data-journalist
honestly admitted its beige table slides lack.

**GRAFT 3, from field-documentarian. NO PEOPLE, hard rule.** No figure, no
silhouette, no hand, no scrubs, no empty chair. An empty chair is a person
shaped hole and reads sentimental. Objects and light only. Also avoids
implying a patient whose consent we do not have.

**REJECTED, field-documentarian's evening timecode.** Making the deck's own
runtime exactly 54 minutes is clever and it is a private joke wearing a
data-in-art costume. It also risks implying the 54 minutes is per evening,
which is the one thing that is false. Cut.

**REJECTED, systems-illustrator's contact-shadow provenance.** Genuinely good,
but this deck already encodes provenance three ways (spec plate, dash state,
fill state). A fourth semantic shadow system is one rule too many for a reader
to learn, and the lens itself worried a device needing a legend fails at
432px.

**REJECTED, field-documentarian's cover.** Putting "12 minutes a visit. 54
minutes a week." in the headline is the exact adjacency the fact-checker
flagged as kill worthy. Naming both bases makes it survivable, not wise. The
data-journalist's cover states the two RULERS rather than two savings, which
makes the same collision without ever presenting either figure as a result.

# NEW TECHNIQUES TO LOG IF THEY WORK

**NEW: Raking Desk Plane.** A lamplit chart-paper plane lying on a cabinet
extruded desk, keyed by one warm off-canvas lamp and rimmed by one cool
off-canvas monitor practical, with a single lifted paper curl as the one
genuinely raised object. All quantities are drawn as flat ink on the
unforeshortened paper, so the deck gets a lit dimensional scene AND keeps
parallel projection for every number. Display type is zoned into the dark room
band above the paper, which solves headline contrast permanently without
scrims. Generalises to any deck whose subject is a document, a measurement, a
ledger, or a report. D3

**NEW: Unit-Collision Ruler with Broken-Scale Callout.** A true-scale baseline
carrying two incommensurable quantities, plus ONE magnified window in which
they briefly share a scale, joined by a drafting break zigzag, with the
magnification factor printed and a mono guard reading SAME SCALE INSIDE THIS
BOX ONLY. DIFFERENT TIME BASES. NOT A SUM. Rules that make it honest, print
the factor, use the standard break symbol, and never let the magnified view be
the slide's only view of the quantity. Generalises to any deck where two
honest numbers disagree because of units, time bases, or populations. D3

**NEW: The Ghost Multiplication.** An arithmetic the reader is about to do in
their head, drawn as repeated phantom-dash copies of a unit bar marching along
a true-scale baseline and FADING through ordered dither to nothing, terminated
by a dashed cap and a question tick, labeled with the input that was never
published. It shows the multiplication and its own impossibility in one
figure, and it implies no count because the fade IS the uncertainty.
Generalises to any per-unit vendor figure a reader will instinctively scale up.
D2

**NEW: The Column That Never Changes.** A table whose argument is a repeated
cell. N rows, N different figures, and the sourcing column reads identically
all the way down. The repetition is the editorial, and the headline names it
as intentional before the eye arrives. Generalises to any single-sourced
evidence set. D1

**NEW: Die-Cut Paper Choropleth.** A region map printed on a sheet of chart
paper lying on a lit desk, where the ZERO category is punched clean through
the sheet rather than filled, so key light passes through and lands on the
surface below. Absence is drawn as absence and reads at thumb size as "there
is nothing there." Rules, punched cells need a lit bevel on the leading edge
or they read as ink blots, always print the count as a mono chip so the thumb
read never depends on counting holes, and NEVER punch a category meaning
"low", only one meaning "none", or the metaphor lies. Generalises to any
coverage, access, service-desert, or presence-absence map. D2
