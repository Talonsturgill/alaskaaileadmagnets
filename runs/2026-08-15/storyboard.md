# DECK HEADER

**Thesis.** A 200 person family trades company put a machine on its after hours
phone, kept its own people on the day shift, and stopped paying anybody to sit
up all night waiting for a call.

**Document title (PDF, 60 char max).** Case File 002, The After Hours Phone

**Arc.** artifact hook (the bill) → identification (a 1958 Akron trades shop) →
the bottleneck made concrete (8,000 a month on top of a 24/7 desk) → the
mechanism and the seven second line → the seller's own number against his own
product → the receipts as a bound → the plan and its honest holes → Anchorage
and the cold snap → the thesis sealed as a shape. Emotional temperature lifts
at slide 5 (the reader is handed a weapon for their next demo) and lands at 8.

**Slide count rationale.** Nine. Eight is the default grammar, and this case
carries one payload that cannot be folded into another slide without losing it,
the vendor's admission that full autonomy books worse (c13). It gets slide 5 to
itself as the curriculum beat, which is also the deck's breather between two
dense slides.

   `#FFC72C` appears ONLY at the line where custody changes hands, budgeted to
