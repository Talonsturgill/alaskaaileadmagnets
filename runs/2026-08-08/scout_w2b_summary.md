## THE ONE GENUINELY NEW THING THAT WORKED — the owner's own newsletter
`colonyfoods.com/about/news/` is a **monthly archive going back to May 2024,
written under Joey Barbagallo's own byline on his own domain.** The August 2025
issue independently describes the same Choco deployment the vendor page
describes, in his own voice, with operator lines the vendor page does not have:
"They're not in their cars punching in orders anymore" and "We're not paying reps
to enter orders. We pay them to build relationships."

That is real corroboration, it is fetchable, and **it is the shape to hunt: a
non-technology mid-market company that publishes a monthly owner-written
newsletter.** Still no price, but it is the only source type in two waves that
gave us an operator describing his own deployment without a vendor holding the
pen.
