## HARD FINDING 1 — the fetch wall
**403 to this fetcher:** BNP Media (achrnews.com, roofingcontractor.com),
forconstructionpros.com, www.mcaa.org. Those are precisely the outlets carrying
mid-market contractor AI stories with named operators.
**The single most valuable thing the next scout can know: `dev.mcaa.org` is a
readable mirror of `www.mcaa.org`** and served the full Smart Solutions case
study index.
**Readable:** constructiondive.com, homepros.news, hvac-blog.acca.org,
upsmith.com, company .com sites.

## HARD FINDING 2 — cost-language queries are dead craft
Queries containing "AI" plus "cost" plus a trade return almost nothing but
"Best AI X Software 2026" SEO spam farms. The queries that surfaced real
operators named a trade outlet or association and used plain reporting language.
**Next scout on this ground should lead with allowed_domains on every search.**

## HARD FINDING 3 — where cost actually lives
Trade press reports outcomes and never asks what it ran. Cost showed up only
**operator-to-operator**: an independent newsletter (homepros.news) and a
podcast episode title. This confirms and sharpens the ECONOMICS.md prior:
**hunt operator newsletters and podcast EPISODE TITLES, because the dollar
figure is often in the title even when the transcript is unreachable.**
