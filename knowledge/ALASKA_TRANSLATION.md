# The Alaska Translation

The differentiator layer. Every case file ends by mapping the win to
Anchorage and Southcentral, honestly. This file carries the rules, the twin
map, and the local proof. Grounded in research/ALASKA_GROUND.md and the
public employer record, refresh both as the market moves.

## Translation rules (never bend)

1. Segments, not targets. The translation speaks to "independent clinics" or
   "air cargo operators," never "you, named local business, should buy this."
   Named local companies appear only as public record sector anchors (the
   Corporate 100 is public) or when their story is already public.
2. Honest rung. The translation names the feasibility ladder rung and the
   rough cost class at mid market scale, per HONEST_AI.md, ranges only.
3. Where it does NOT translate. Every translation names at least one limit,
   scale, data, regulation, season, or the plain software answer. Alaska
   bans finfish farming, so aquaculture CV is an analogy here, say so.
4. No product names of ours. The translation teaches the build shape, the
   offer line does the only selling, and it is configured elsewhere.
5. The why-now is the labor frame. Thirteen straight years of net out
   migration, roughly a quarter of the workforce nonresident, extreme
   seasonality. AI is how an Alaska business does more with the workers it
   can actually get. Use it as frame, verify before quoting numbers.

## Local proof (the stories that make it real here, sources in research/)

- SalmonVision counts salmon by computer vision at about 16 Alaska and BC
  sites, the Sitka Tribe kept the Redoubt Lake weir counting after funding
  cuts, about 90 percent reported accuracy, an hour of video in about a
  minute.
- NOAA's Alaska science center trained a small YOLO model on under 17,000
  labeled frames and cut bycatch review from days or weeks to hours. That is
  a mid market sized build running on the state's biggest fishery.
- Plow route optimization saved Indiana DOT about 5 million dollars and cut
  Iowa deadhead 13.2 percent, Anchorage already publishes a live plow map,
  the optimization layer is the unclaimed win.
- The honest counterweight always available, Kenai sonar echogram counting
  still ran about 23 percent error in 2024 research, advancing, not solved.
